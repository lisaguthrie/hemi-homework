Seeing the Light
In 1803, Thomas Young made a discovery about light. He found that when light from two sources overlapped, it made a pattern of bright light and darkness. He thought light acted like a wave: the bright areas were created when two light waves matched up; the dark areas were created when two light waves did not match. His theory led to future discoveries about light.
